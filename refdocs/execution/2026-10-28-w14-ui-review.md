# Execution: W14, a UI review of the running app

> Plan: `refdocs/plans/2026-10-28-w14-ui-review.md`. Targeted tests while building, one combined verification at the end.

## How the review was run

A scratch script started the API (fakes, Malay drafts shown) and the built web app on spare ports (8031 and 3031) against a private store seeded with six Plans and their Malay translations, verified for that origin, and took screenshots of the main states; a second script scrolled inside the lab frame. Port 3000 was held by a server the assistant did not start, and it was left alone.

## What was found, and what was done

| Found | Fixed |
|---|---|
| The library's labs were refused (`csp_mismatch`) and the fake pipeline answered instead, with the wrong badge | Not a product bug: the library was verified for `http://localhost:3000` and the review ran on 3031; the gate was right. The review store is seeded for its own origin |
| At 360 px the top bar overflowed: "My progress" wrapped and the theme toggle was cut off (the smoke passed because the page clips rather than scrolls) | The wordmark text hides below 420 px, the progress button does not wrap, the draft chip hides on narrow screens |
| The question hint wrapped and was cut in half in the narrow conversation column | The hint stays on one line with an ellipsis |
| "Draf terjemahan" was shown twice (a full-width bar and the lab's note) | A small "Draf" chip in the top bar; the lab keeps its note |
| The "Your lab bench" heading took 60 px once a lab was open | Shown only while the bench is empty; the frame is 11rem shorter than the viewport instead of 15rem |
| The Hooke masses were at the bottom of the bench, below the spring and the graph | The masses sit beside the spring on a wide page; the graphs move to the right column when the bench already has a picture |
| The right column was empty under the table, the left one very long | As above: graphs in the right column |
| The predict card sat under the whole bench, though the student is to predict first | It is in the header, after the key idea, above the bench, at every width |
| A phone landed on the exam card after a question, past the lab | Once the lab arrives on a narrow screen the conversation brings it to the top |
| The Peek frame on a phone showed only the lab's header | The frame is `min(75dvh, 640px)` tall |
| In dark mode the apparatus outlines (burette, flask, tubes, the spring's pan, the cannon's arrow) were near black on a near black bench | A line-art colour of its own (`--syn-line`), light in dark mode |

## Deviations and facts worth keeping

- **The verifier caught a real phone defect.** After the graphs moved, the fermentation dial was refused (`dial_dead`): on a narrow page the bench's sentence sits above the dial, it changes length as the dial turns, and the browser's scroll anchoring kept the sentence still and moved the knob under the finger. Two changes: the Kit's bench is excluded from scroll anchoring (`overflow-anchor: none`), so the knob stays put; and the harness measures the knob again before each turn (strictly more accurate, not weaker).
- The predict card was first put at the top of the right column; that was not the cause of the dial failure, but in the header it is in the right place at every width, so it stayed there.
- Deleting scratch files was refused by the permission settings; the review stores are in the system temp folder.
