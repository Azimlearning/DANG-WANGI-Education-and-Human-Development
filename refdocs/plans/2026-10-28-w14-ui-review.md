# Plan: W14, a UI review of the running app, and the fixes it found

> Written 2026-10-28, after the owner asked to "go back to focus on synapse v4 dev, check the ui also". Execution: `refdocs/execution/2026-10-28-w14-ui-review.md`. Decision: D-64.

## What and why

The smoke checks numbers (widths, heights, counts); it does not look. This block ran the real app on a seeded library and looked at it, in English and Malay, light and dark, at 1280 x 800 and 360 x 780, with the lab scrolled inside its frame, and fixed what a student would trip over.

## Scope

**In:** the lab page's layout (where the graphs, the predict card and the masses sit), the frame's height, the top bar at 360 px, the question box hint, the draft mark, where a phone lands after a question, the Peek height, the colour of scene line art in dark mode, and any verifier change these need.

**Out:** the predict card as an overlay (a behaviour change, D-60), illustrated scenes for the labs with only a graph, the library's chat words repeating the lab's key idea (the words are the Plan's note; changing them is a content decision), a `kit-guardian` review.

## Exit criteria

- At 1280 x 800 the bench, its controls and its first readout are in view without scrolling the frame, for the four demo labs; the Hooke masses sit beside the spring.
- At 360 px the top bar fits in both languages and a phone lands on the lab, not past it.
- Scene line art is readable in dark mode.
- The verifier, the L6 baseline and the hostile set are unchanged in result.
