# Plan: W18, make the benches and the loading feel like V3 and the demo video

> Written 2026-10-29, from the owner's review of the running app. Execution: `refdocs/execution/2026-10-29-w18-v3-feel.md`. Decisions: D-72 to D-74.

## What the owner reported

1. A hydration error in the browser console on `<body>` (Next.js 16).
2. The Hooke's law bench is not drag and drop as in V3 and the demo video: the masses sit in a "tray" and a "hanger" box far from the spring, the spring is a bare zigzag, and a slider shows beside a drag-and-drop control that already does the job.
3. Newton's cannonball is far poorer than V3's: V3 has a Launch button, a ball that flies along its path, traces of earlier launches and a dark sky; V4 draws a still schematic.
4. Some prompts do not work; only the library's demo experiments do.
5. The multi-stage loading bar of V3 and the demo video "is not there anymore".

## Diagnosed

- 1: a browser extension (Grammarly) adds `data-gr-*` attributes to `<body>` before React loads. Not a bug in the page.
- 4: the server runs on `SYNAPSE_LLM_MODE=fake` (the default; `[fake:explain:...]` appears in the chat). The fake model can reply only to what the stored library holds, so any other question ends as "Explanation only". The page never said so.
- 5: the bar exists (`ProgressStages`) but (a) a stored lab and the fake model finish in under half a second so it flashed by, (b) on a wide screen it was replaced by the plan's draft card as soon as the plan arrived, and (c) a turn that ended with no lab dropped the bench back to "Ready when you are", as if nothing had happened.

## What to build

1. `suppressHydrationWarning` on `<body>` (the `<html>` already has it).
2. Web: the bar is paced (a stage at a time, at least 280 ms each, only while the server is faster than that), stays above the draft, and the lab replaces it when it reaches "Ready". A turn that ends with no lab shows a card saying why. `/health` reports `llm_mode`; a "Demo model" chip and the no-lab card say plainly that the server is on the fake model.
3. Kit, Hooke: the spring scene draws a retort stand, a ruler with a pointer and a cord to the hanger, and goes inside the mass bench above the hanger, so the masses are dropped on what hangs from the spring and stack as slotted discs. The range input stays on the page (the sweep, keyboard and screen reader use it) but is drawn only when focused.
4. Kit, orbit: a dark starry sky, a Launch button (a registered Kit action), a cannonball that flies once (fall, escape) or twice round (circle, oval), faint traces of the last three launches, path colours by outcome. A new speed ends a flight in the air; reduced motion skips the flight.
5. Prompts that fall outside the library: needs `SYNAPSE_LLM_MODE=live` (a paid call per question, about $0.03 to $0.10 each by the W16 measurement). Not turned on by this workstream; the owner decides.

## Done means

- Library reseeded on the relocked Kit, all `kit-verified`; Kit, web and API tests pass; L6 refuses its planted defects with no false positive; the hostile set 20 of 20.
- The Hooke and orbit benches looked at in a real browser, light and dark, wide and 360 px.

## Not in this workstream

Animating the spring's stretch between positions (the audit compares the drawn attributes to the model's number at every instant, so a tween needs its own contract); a "planets and gravity" bench (a Plan for the live planner or a new Kit part); a paid measurement of arbitrary prompts.
