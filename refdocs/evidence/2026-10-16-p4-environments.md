## What this does and does not show

- It shows the machinery works across all three subjects: a new science model plus a Plan becomes a verified, interactive Environment with no
  page-writing by a model. The planner (an LLM) has never been run on these; that is the first paid run's job (see the cost projection).
- Every label is `model-checked` and every citation is UNVERIFIED. The formulas were written by hand and checked against independent hand-written
  references and planted-bug suites, but no textbook page has confirmed them or the wording, units or scope of each experiment. The corpus (P2) is
  loaded locally by the owner; until then these are demonstrations, not curriculum content.
- The Biology photosynthesis curve is a stylised teaching curve, not data, and says so on the page.
- The experiments are named from the KSSM topic list and general knowledge of SPM practicals. The owner's experiment catalogue (P2) should replace or
  confirm each one.
