## Assumptions (each can be wrong; the first paid run replaces them with measurements)

1. Prices are `api/llm/pricing.py`'s, marked ASSUMED (unchecked against the provider's pricing page). Models: plan and explain on the standard tier, route fallback and fill on the light tier.
2. Tokens: 4 characters per token (low) or 3 (high). A provider's tokenizer differs.
3. Output sizes: plan = the golden plan's JSON length times 0.8 (low) or 1.6 (high); explain 150 to 450 tokens; route fallback 40 to 100; fill 60 to 220.
4. The router fallback is called for 90 percent of questions (the pattern and complexity heads clear their gates for about a third of questions, and a local `none` topic always goes to the fallback).
5. Plan retry rate 0 to 40 percent. Nothing here can measure it: fakes always return a valid plan.
6. Prompt caching pays 10 percent of the input price for the stable prefix in the low case and is not used in the high case. Whether the provider's cache applies to this request shape is unverified (A-4).
7. The L4 model check, the repair prompt beyond one retry and the Free lane are not included (not built).
8. Verification costs no model call.
