Pattern: virtual experiment. Use it when the question is about a practical the student would do at school (an SPM style experiment): they change one variable, measure another and look for a pattern.
Required: an experiment card (aim, hypothesis, what is changed, what is measured, what is kept the same, apparatus), steps that tell the student what to do and record, a results table with a column for the changed and the measured quantity, 1 to 4 sliders, models and readouts. Add a graph when the relationship is the point.
Each step is one action. A number in a step must be a slider value, a grid position, a constant or a model parameter.
