Pattern: simulation. Use it when the student should explore how one quantity depends on one or two others by moving sliders and watching a readout and a graph.
Required: 1 to 4 sliders, 1 to 3 models, 1 to 4 readouts. Add one graph of the readout against the main slider when the relationship is the point. Add one or two sentences that tell the student what to try.
Do not use an experiment card or a results table in this pattern.
