@@TITLE
Pressure and depth
@@KICKER
Physics, Form 5
@@TRY
Drag the diver down into the water and watch the pressure readout climb.
@@IDEA
The pressure from a liquid grows with depth: p = h x rho x g.
Here rho is 1000 kg/m3 for water and g is 9.81 m/s2.
@@PREDICT
The diver goes twice as deep. What happens to the pressure from the water?
- It halves
- It stays the same
- It doubles
Check: drag the diver from 2 m to 4 m and compare the two pressures.
@@STEPS
- Drag the diver to 2 m and read the pressure.
- Drag the diver to 4 m and read it again.
- Compare the two readings.
@@HINTS
- Pressure depends on how much water is above the diver.
- Look at how the pressure changes when the depth doubles.
@@EXPLAIN
The water above the diver pushes down with its weight. Twice the depth means twice the water above, so twice the pressure from the water.
@@STYLE
.water{fill:#7cc4d8}
@@DRAWING
<svg id="tank" viewBox="0 0 200 240" role="img" aria-label="A tank of water with a diver at 2 metres">
  <rect class="water" x="20" y="20" width="160" height="200"/>
  <circle id="diver" cx="100" cy="60" r="24" fill="#d6336c" data-synapse-handle="depth" aria-hidden="true" style="touch-action:none;cursor:grab"/>
</svg>
@@CONTROLS
depth | Depth | 0 | 10 | 0.5 | 2 | m
@@READOUTS
pressure | Pressure from the water (kPa)
@@SCRIPT
var RHO_KG_PER_M3 = 1000;
var G_M_PER_S2 = 9.81;
var depth = document.getElementById("depth");
var out = document.getElementById("pressure");
var found = document.getElementById("found");
var tank = document.getElementById("tank");
var diver = document.getElementById("diver");
function pressureKpa(hMetres) { return hMetres * RHO_KG_PER_M3 * G_M_PER_S2 / 1000; }
function show() {
  var h = Number(depth.value);
  out.textContent = pressureKpa(h).toFixed(1) + " kPa";
  diver.setAttribute("cy", String(30 + h * 18));
  tank.setAttribute("aria-label", "A tank of water with a diver at " + h + " metres");
  found.textContent = "At " + h + " m the water pushes with " + pressureKpa(h).toFixed(1) + " kPa.";
}
var held = false;
diver.addEventListener("pointerdown", function (e) { held = true; diver.setPointerCapture(e.pointerId); });
diver.addEventListener("pointermove", function (e) {
  if (!held) return;
  var r = tank.getBoundingClientRect();
  var f = Math.min(1, Math.max(0, (e.clientY - r.top) / r.height));
  depth.value = String(Math.round(f * 20) / 2);
  depth.dispatchEvent(new Event("input", { bubbles: true }));
});
diver.addEventListener("pointerup", function () { held = false; });
depth.addEventListener("input", show);
show();
@@END
