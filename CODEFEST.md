# UTP CodeFest '26

This repository is the entry of **Team Dang Wangi** in **Track 2, Education and Human Development**, at UTP CodeFest '26.

**Project:** Synapse v4. A Form 4 or 5 student asks a science question and gets an interactive experiment built for it, checked in a real browser before they see it, with its source, trust label and limits shown beside it. It answers the practical-lab access gap in Malaysian secondary schools, where many students read about an experiment without being able to run it.

**Team:** Fakhrul Azim, Abdul Hafiz, Nur Farah, Por Jie Hao.
